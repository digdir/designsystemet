### Patch Changes

- **Date and time pattern:** Nuanced the guidance on date pickers. A custom-built date picker is now presented as an option when the browser's built-in `type="date"` does not cover the need. ([#4985](https://github.com/digdir/designsystemet/pull/4985))

- Links to files, like `/llms.txt` or a page's `.md` version, now open when clicked (thanks @tajakobsen) ([#5677](https://github.com/digdir/designsystemet/pull/5677))
