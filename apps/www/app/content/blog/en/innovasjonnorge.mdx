Innovation Norway has built its own component library, [Koble](https://koble.innovasjonnorge.no/?path=/docs/introduction-hva-er-koble--docs), with Designsystemet as its foundation. Today, most of Innovation Norway's solutions and services use Koble, and older solutions are gradually being moved over.

We asked Innovation Norway a few questions about why they chose to build on Designsystemet, how they use it today, and what they have learned along the way.

## Why did Innovation Norway start using Designsystemet?

We were well underway with building Koble, our own component library. Our main focus had first been components for [innovasjonnorge.no](https://www.innovasjonnorge.no), and then for our internal pages. When we needed to cover the needs of more product teams, we saw that many of the components already existed in Designsystemet. This gave us the opportunity to develop Koble further on a well-designed, shared public foundation, while keeping the freedom to adapt and build components for our own needs.

For us, the value of Koble is about more than components and code. We want to establish a shared toolbox, a shared language and a shared direction for digital user experiences at Innovation Norway. A foundation like this helps us meet requirements, maintain quality and follow good practice, and makes it easier for product teams to collaborate, share knowledge and build on each other's work.

By using Designsystemet, we benefit from ongoing improvements and new features, and can spend more of our time on what is specific to our services. In this way, Koble is also a long-term investment in how we develop digital services.

It was also good to be able to rely on something that is meant to be a standard across public sector organisations.

## Where and how do you use Designsystemet today?

We use Designsystemet as the foundation for our own component library, Koble. Most of our solutions and services use Koble today, and we have ongoing work to migrate older solutions over to Koble.

Examples of services are Nettbank, Min side and Prosjekthjelpen.

## How have you adapted Designsystemet to Innovation Norway's visual identity and needs?

We have adapted the colours and generated our own tokens with help from the [theme builder](https://theme.designsystemet.no/en). Where necessary, we have also adjusted some colours in the colour scale manually.

In addition, we have adjusted the font size and typeface, as well as the radius of some components.

Our `Button` component has a different visual expression, but uses `Button` from Designsystemet as its foundation. When we create new components, we also use Designsystemet as the starting point.

## What do you see as the biggest benefits of using a shared design system as a foundation?

- A solid and good foundation for components, which meets many of the requirements we face, while being flexible enough for us to adapt it to our own needs.
- Good guidelines, documentation and patterns we can rely on.
- It is open source, and we can find inspiration from others who also use Designsystemet as their foundation.
- A very professional, friendly and skilled team building Designsystemet.

## What has been the most challenging, or what have you had to solve yourselves?

It was actually surprisingly easy to bring Designsystemet into our existing library and align it with our own setup.

What we need to work on is migrating components we already had, which do not exist in Designsystemet, so that they use the same structure and tokens as the components based on Designsystemet. It is not a crisis, but it can sometimes be confusing for those who use the library.

## What would you like from Designsystemet going forward?

Keep up the fantastic work! A bonus would be more good patterns and guidelines. They are very valuable and help us develop our solutions and services.

We have made some scripts to stay in sync with Designsystemet's documentation. It works fine, but it is not always easy to maintain when we have our own guidelines in some cases.
