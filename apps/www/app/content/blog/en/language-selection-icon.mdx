### Methodological limitations
Lacks a clear and thorough qualitative study. Observations only say something about what is used today, not how well it works or whether the designers made their choice out of habit or through a proper process. The basis for decisions is therefore limited to what we can observe, 2 association tests, and various professional articles that either address this icon problem directly or iconography more generally.

## What we landed on
The language choice pattern describes what is recommended, for example:
- Language icon + visible text to identify the language choice
- Language names in the language itself
- Flags are not used to identify a language or a language choice
- Make it clear what the language choice applies to
- User preferences as help, but not as a replacement for the user's own choice
- correct code and language markup in the implementation
