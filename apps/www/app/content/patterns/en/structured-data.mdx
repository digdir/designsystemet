Many public sector services use tables to present structured information. On large screens, tables often provide a good overview and make it easy to compare information. On small screens, space is more limited, and the same table layout can become difficult to read and use. How can we present structured information in a way that also works well on small screens?

In autumn 2026, a cross-agency working group is exploring a shared pattern for structured information on small screens. Among other things, the working group is looking at how data can be presented in a clear and accessible way while preserving important information and relationships.

Do you have experience, user testing, or other relevant findings in this area? Please share them in the [GitHub discussion (github.com)](https://github.com/digdir/designsystemet/discussions/3703). Your input will become part of the knowledge base for this work.
