A calendar (date picker) is not always the best solution when users need to provide a date. Simple text fields, separate fields for day, month and year, or the browser’s built-in support for date input often work better. 

At the same time, the browser’s built-in date picker (`type="date"`) does not cover every need, and we therefore expect to provide our own date picker in the future. 

We do not have one yet, but we provide guidance on [how to ask users for date and time](/en/patterns/date-and-time).

<Card data-color='info'>
