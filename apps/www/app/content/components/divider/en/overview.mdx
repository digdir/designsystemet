**Use divider when**
- you want to create a clear separation between sections of content
- spacing alone does not provide sufficient separation

**Avoid divider when**
- natural spacing or other visual elements provide enough separation
- too many divider elements can create a cluttered visual appearance

## Guidelines

Divider is used to group content that belongs together, making it easier to read and understand. It can also be used to separate content that is related but still benefits from a visual distinction.

Consider whether divider works well with the other visual elements you use, so the overall experience feels tidy and consistent.
