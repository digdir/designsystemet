**Avatar:** Removed deprecated props and attributes:
- `variant="square"` and `data-variant="square"`: use `--dsc-avatar-radius`
- `initials` and `data-initials`: place the initials as text inside the avatar
- `data-size="xs"`: use `sm`, `md` or `lg`, or set `--dsc-avatar-size`
