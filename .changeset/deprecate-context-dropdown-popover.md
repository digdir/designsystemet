**Dropdown, Popover:** Deprecates `TriggerContext` and `Trigger`, which will be removed in the next major version. Use native [Popover API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Popover_API/Using) on `Button` with `popovertarget` set to the popover's or dropdown's `id`.
